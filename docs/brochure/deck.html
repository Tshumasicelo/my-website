What is ASPECTS

One app for your money
and your momentum.

Most budgeting apps only look backwards. ASPECTS pairs a money tracker with small daily goals, so every day you know where you stand and what to do next.

💰

Track your money

Income and expenses in seconds, with categories, budgets and charts.

  • Monthly budgets per category
  • Recurring salary, rent and subscriptions
  • Your full history, month by month
✅

Grow your habits

Break big plans into micro-tasks you can finish today, and stay focused.

  • Priorities, due dates and reminders
  • A built-in 25-minute focus timer
  • Daily streaks and levels
🎯

Save for goals

Set targets like a laptop or a trip and watch the progress rings fill.

  • Move money in or out at any time
  • Estimated time to reach each goal
  • A celebration when you get there 🎉
